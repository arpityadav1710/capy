# Art prompts for Capy and the visitors

Tool: **Google Gemini** (free) — it keeps the same character across pictures when you give it a reference.
Backup: ChatGPT (free tier, fewer pictures per day).

## How to use these
1. Open Gemini in one chat and keep using that same chat for everything, so the style stays consistent.
2. Attach `design/capy-face-reference.png` (Capy's face) with the first prompt.
3. Paste **Style** + one item prompt. Generate, and regenerate until it looks right.
4. For the visitors, also attach your favourite Capy picture from step 3 so they match.
5. Remove the white background: on your iPhone, open the picture in Photos, press and hold the animal until it glows, then **Share → Save Image** (or **Copy**). That gives a see-through PNG. (On the laptop: Adobe Express "Remove background", free.)
6. Save each file with the name shown, and send them to me; I'll put them in `assets/art/` and animate them.

## Style (paste before every prompt)
> Soft, cozy kawaii illustration in the exact style of the attached capybara: warm caramel-tan fur with a gentle painterly texture, round chubby shapes, rosy peach blush on the cheeks, a big rounded dark-brown nose with a small Y-shaped mouth, eyes drawn as short dark curved strokes, no hard black outlines, soft warm lighting, warm pastel palette. Plain pure white background, the character centered with space around it, nothing else in the frame, no text, no shadow on the ground, square 1:1.

## Visitors
Make visitors in a **Temporary chat** in Gemini (or ChatGPT), **without the Capy picture attached**, and never mention "capybara" in the prompt — even "no capybara" makes it draw one.
Paste **Style**, then this line, then the visitor's description:
> Draw one small animal on its own, facing the viewer. Keep its real anatomy for its species (a bird has a beak and feathers, an insect has a slim body and antennae, and so on), just drawn cute and slightly chubby. The animal:

Once one visitor comes out right, attach **that visitor** (not Capy) as the style reference for the rest.

## Tips
- If Capy comes out different, say: "Keep the exact same capybara as the first picture, only change the pose."
- If outlines appear, add: "no black outlines".
- Make a few a day if the free limit runs out; there's no rush.
- Only use art you generated yourself (not the sticker sheet from Pinterest), since the GitHub repo is public.
